


MUSCLE WEAPON®

UI / UX DESIGN SPECIFICATION

Light • Futuristic • Athlete-first • Premium • 2050-inspired


Implementation reference for Antigravity. This document defines the visual system, page layouts, components, states, interactions and responsive behavior for the Muscle Weapon website.


1. Design Direction
The experience should feel like a premium sports-tech platform rather than a conventional supplement store. The visual direction is intentionally LIGHT: white and cool-gray surfaces, deep navy typography, electric blue accents, crisp product photography, subtle glass effects, generous whitespace and controlled 3D depth.
Principle
Design rule
Athlete-first
Every major section should communicate performance, discipline, recovery or authenticity.
Light futuristic
Use white/light-gray backgrounds with blue energy accents; avoid an all-black interface.
Premium clarity
Large typography, clean hierarchy, strong product imagery and restrained decoration.
3D depth
Use soft shadows, floating cards, subtle perspective and product hero renders without visual clutter.
Fast comprehension
Primary CTA and product information must be obvious within 1–2 seconds.
Real data only
Production UI must render database data; never populate visible production screens with fake metrics/products.

4. Global Navigation &amp; Footer
Header: white translucent surface, 1px bottom border, compact Muscle Weapon logo at left, navigation in the center, search/cart/account actions at right. On scroll, header becomes sticky with a subtle blur and shadow.
Primary navigation: Home • Products • About • Verify Product • Blogs • Contact. Admin navigation is separate and never exposed as a public link.
Footer: logo, short brand statement, product category links, customer support, Amazon/Flipkart links, social links, legal links and copyright. Keep the footer white with navy text and blue hover accents.

6. User Pages


6.1 Home Page
Hero: full-width light visual with premium supplement/product render on one side and a bold performance headline on the other.Primary CTA: Shop Products. Secondary CTA: Verify Product.Immediately below: trust strip with Authentic Products, Premium Quality, Athlete Focus, Fast Support.Then: Shop by Category using seven category tiles: Protein, Mass Gainer, Multivitamins, Calcium, Omega Gold Fish Oil, Creatine, Pre-Workout.Then: Featured Products populated only from products marked visible by Admin.Then: brand-performance campaign banner with athlete imagery.Then: benefits/trust section, followed by support CTA and footer.


6.2 Products Page
Two-column desktop layout: left filter rail, right product grid. Top row contains title, product count from database and sort control.Product cards use white surfaces, subtle border/shadow, strong product photography and blue CTA.Filters: category, price, flavor, availability and any product attributes actually stored.Mobile: filter opens as a bottom sheet/drawer. No fake products or invented ratings.


6.3 Product Detail
Breadcrumbs at top. Main area uses a 2-column gallery + purchase panel.Purchase panel: product name, real rating/review count if available, price, stock state, variant/flavor selectors, quantity, Add to Cart and Buy Now.Below: tabs/sections for Description, Nutrition Facts, Ingredients, How to Use, Reviews.Show authenticity/trust icons only when supported by actual product/business information.


6.4 About
Editorial brand story page. Large athlete image/visual, mission statement, brand principles, quality approach and a clear route to Products.Avoid invented statistics such as customer counts or ratings unless those values exist in the database.


6.5 Verify Product
Focused authenticity experience.Step 1: enter mandatory valid mobile number.Step 2: enter scratch code.Step 3: submit verification.Success state shows verified/not-previously-verified status and safe product information available for that code.Failure states clearly explain invalid, disabled, already verified or rate-limited scenarios.Do not expose sensitive verification logs to users.


6.6 Contact
Two-column layout: support information on the left and contact form on the right.Support email: muscle.weapon@gmail.com.Phone: 8816090309.Form: name, email, mobile where appropriate, subject, message and consent/anti-spam control.Show success confirmation only after the submission is accepted by the backend.


7. Admin Experience
The Admin interface should feel substantially more powerful than the public site: dense but clean information architecture, persistent sidebar, command/search capability, real-time status indicators, filters, bulk actions and contextual drawers.


7.1 Admin Login
Secure authentication, clear error states, password visibility control, session handling and optional MFA if configured.


7.2 Dashboard
Real-time metrics from Supabase only. Product count, visible products, verification activity and other actual business metrics. Charts must not render fabricated values.


7.3 Products
Search/filter/table view with image, name, category, price, stock/visibility status, updated date and actions.


7.4 Add/Edit Product
Structured form for product identity, category, pricing, variants, specifications, nutrition facts, ingredients, usage, SEO fields, visibility and images. Images upload to Cloudinary; metadata and URLs stored in Supabase.


7.5 Categories
Create/edit/disable category records; product counts are derived from database relations.


7.6 Verification Codes
Input quantity 1–5000, generate unique codes, status management, product-independent assignment, export XLS/PDF, search/filter, disable/delete where business rules allow.


7.7 Verification Logs
Audit table showing code, verification timestamp, masked/appropriate mobile information, approximate location information when legally/technically available, device/browser metadata and product context. Apply data minimization and retention controls.


7.8 Customers
Only real customer records from the database. Search and filter with privacy-aware fields.


7.9 Reviews
Moderation interface for real reviews if reviews are implemented. Include status and moderation actions.


7.10 Content/Banners
Manage homepage campaigns, banners and visibility without requiring code changes.


7.11 Settings
Admin profile, roles/permissions, security, integrations and operational settings.

9. Motion &amp; 3D Language
Use motion to communicate hierarchy, not decoration. Hero products can float 6–12px on a slow loop; cards may lift 2–4px on hover; page sections use short 250–450ms reveal transitions. Avoid excessive parallax, particle effects or continuous animation that reduces readability or performance. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
3D direction: realistic product renders, soft studio shadows, controlled reflections and subtle depth. Keep the background light. Use blue glows sparingly around primary actions or hero objects.

11. Accessibility
Use semantic headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, accessible labels, sufficient color contrast, alt text for meaningful product images, error messages associated with fields, ARIA only where necessary, and reduced-motion support. Never communicate status by color alone.


12. Data &amp; Implementation Rules


Supabase is the source of truth for application data.


Cloudinary stores product media; store stable Cloudinary URLs/IDs in Supabase.


Public product pages show only records explicitly marked visible/published by Admin.


Admin dashboards display only live queried values; no demo metrics in production.


All privileged operations require server-side authorization and role checks.


Verification codes must be cryptographically generated and unique; uniqueness must also be enforced at the database level.


Exports must be generated from the selected real dataset and reflect the applied filters when applicable.


Delete/disable actions must have explicit confirmation and auditability.


Use pagination for large datasets; do not load thousands of rows into the browser unnecessarily.


Keep secrets and service credentials server-side; never expose Supabase service-role or Cloudinary secrets in client code.

14. Final Visual QA Checklist
☐ Light background remains dominant; interface must not drift into an all-dark theme.
☐ Muscle Weapon logo remains visually consistent and is never stretched.
☐ Blue is used as an accent/interaction color, not as a full-page background.
☐ Product imagery is sharp, clean and consistently framed.
☐ Primary CTA is visually obvious on every conversion-oriented page.
☐ Desktop layouts have deliberate whitespace and do not look like generic templates.
☐ Admin screens prioritize information density while preserving hierarchy.
☐ All production numbers, products, ratings and verification records originate from real data.
☐ Loading, empty, error and success states are designed before implementation.
☐ Mobile layouts are intentionally designed rather than simply shrinking desktop screens.


15. Handoff Note for Antigravity
Treat this document as the visual/interaction contract. Build reusable components from the design tokens first, then implement the public shell, product system, verification flow and admin shell. Do not invent additional visual systems page-by-page. When a requirement is not specified, preserve the same light premium visual language and choose the simplest accessible interaction.
